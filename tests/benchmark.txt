# Benchmark sentences for the CCG lambda. parser
# Lines starting with '#' or blank lines are ignored.
# Each sentence must parse to at least one S or Q result.

# --- Basic quantifiers ---
Every student likes a cat
Some person sees every dog
No cat likes a dog
A student likes every cat
Every dog runs
Some cat sleeps

# --- Proper names ---
john likes a cat
mary sees every student
alice runs
bob loves mary

# --- The (iota) ---
The student runs
The cat sees the dog
Every student loves the professor
The professor likes the student

# --- Adjectives ---
Every happy student runs
Some tall professor teaches a small class
The brave soldier fights
A young dog sleeps
Every old cat runs

# --- Modals ---
Every student can run
Some cat must sleep
The professor should teach a student
A dog will run
Mary can see john
Every student must like a cat

# --- Negation ---
john not run
john doesn't run
The student doesn't like a cat
Mary cannot see every dog

# --- Do-support negation ---
john does not run
The student does not like a cat

# --- Past tense ---
john liked a cat
Every student saw a dog
The professor taught a student
Mary loved john
A dog ran
Every cat slept

# --- Copula ---
john is a student
Mary is happy
The professor is tall
Every student is brave
Some cat is a dog

# --- Coordination ---
john and mary run
Every student and professor sleeps
A cat or dog runs
john likes mary and alice
Every student studies and sleeps

# --- Relative clauses (subject) ---
Every student that runs sleeps
The professor who teaches a student runs
Every dog that john sees runs
Every student that likes a cat sleeps

# --- Relative clauses (object) ---
A cat that a student likes runs
The professor that mary sees teaches a student
Every book that john reads is good

# --- Wh-questions ---
who runs
who likes a cat
what runs

# --- Ditransitive ---
john gives mary a book
Every professor gives a student a paper
Mary gives john a cat

# --- PP / prepositional ---
Every student runs quickly
john sleeps soundly
Mary studies carefully

# --- Complex sentences ---
Every student that likes a cat sees a dog
Some professor who runs teaches every happy student
The cat that john likes sees the dog that mary loves
Every student can see a professor who teaches a student
john and mary like every cat that runs
